lead: "A satirical political map that charts the regions of Yugoslavia during the decade of its dissolution onto the regions of the contemporary world, in line with today’s geopolitical reality. Subtitle of the map: ‘We were not an atavism but an avant-garde.’ At the bottom of the map: ‘Yugondwana: the dialectics of rotation and revolution.’"

The manifesto that accompanied the publication of the map:

<em>— The whole world is falling apart along our seams.

— From Kamchatka to Chile! From Alaska to New Zealand! Reified by capital, in the middle of the Solar System. Liber-slavia! Neoliberslavia!

— What happened to Yugoslavia in the 1990s – material plundering legitimized by identity wars – now seems to be playing out globally, only this time under the influence of artificial intelligence.

— When I find the time and energy, I’ll turn this map into a globe.

— Our traumas and post-traumatic sensitivities are becoming global traumas and global post-traumatic sensitivities.

— How to parody the Balkan obsession with geopolitics when that very obsession itself is becoming global?

— Tidal waves of neoliberal-nostalgia await us. Monsoon tears of neolib-nostalgics.

— They could learn from us, come here to see their own future, but how, when they are too arrogant and we are too fucked up?

— Every map lies, and this one especially.

— But will you see a better visual analysis?

— Because it is not the world that is falling apart but your mental model of the world.</em> <mark>&#9632;</mark>
